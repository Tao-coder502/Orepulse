# DEMO SCRIPT – Kansanshi Mining PLC AI Hackathon (Strict 5-Minute Delivery)

## 0:00 – 0:30 | The Problem
> *"Good morning judges and Kansanshi Mining leadership. Mining operations in the Zambian Copperbelt are complex, capital-intensive, and safety-critical. When new engineers and operators learn about AI, it is almost always treated as a mysterious black box. You cannot simply let a student or junior engineer experiment with AI inside a live dispatch or fleet control room — the stakes are far too high. This creates a dangerous learning gap."*

## 0:30 – 1:00 | The Solution: OrePulse AI
> *"To bridge this gap, we built **OrePulse AI**: an interactive agentic mining learning laboratory. OrePulse is NOT a generic chatbot and NOT an unvetted autopilot. It is an educational flight simulator for the mining value chain. Learners interact with synthetic operational scenarios, watch specialized AI agents collaborate using Google ADK 2.0, and witness how a deterministic safety engine guarantees that human supervisors always retain absolute authority."*

## 1:00 – 3:15 | Live Demonstration
1. **Scenario Selection**:
   > *"Let's select the scenario: **Combined Operational Risk**."*
2. **Reviewing Synthetic Telemetry**:
   > *"Notice the synthetic telemetry: Haul truck engine temperature is climbing past 110°C, and haul ramp congestion is at 80% with fleet availability degraded to 40%."*
3. **Running the Simulation**:
   > *"We click 'Run Simulation'. Instantly, you see the execution flow across our agent timeline."*
4. **Agent Timeline Breakdown**:
   > *"First, the **Supervisor Agent** coordinates the workflow. It invokes the **Fleet Agent**, which identifies haul road bottlenecks, and the **Maintenance Agent**, which analyzes mechanical thermal stress."*
5. **Deterministic Safety Engine in Action**:
   > *"Here is our core innovation: before any action is accepted, our **authoritative TypeScript Safety Engine** evaluates the proposal. Because multiple critical risk thresholds are breached, the Safety Engine enforces: **STOP & INFORM SUPERVISOR**."*
6. **The Learning Agent**:
   > *"Below, the **Learning Agent** translates this technical trace into an educational reflection: explaining the risk factors and asking the learner to consider how ramp bottlenecks exacerbate equipment overheating."*
7. **Interactive What-If Experiment**:
   > *"Now, let's test a what-if hypothesis. What if dispatch had cleared the haul ramp? We reduce ramp congestion to 20% and rerun. Notice how the safety status immediately changes to NORMAL, visually proving the relationship between traffic flow and thermal management."*

## 3:15 – 4:00 | System Architecture
> *"Architecturally, OrePulse uses a three-tier architecture:*
> 1. *A deterministic simulation engine generating realistic synthetic telemetry.*
> 2. *Google ADK 2.0 agents collaborating via typed contracts.*
> 3. *An uncompromising deterministic safety engine acting as an inviolable guardrail."*

## 4:00 – 4:30 | Responsible AI (Section 9 Compliance)
> *"OrePulse is built from day one around Kansanshi's Responsible AI principles:*
> - *Fairness & Transparency: Every recommendation is explained step-by-step.*
> - *Safety & Privacy: 100% synthetic, anonymized data; zero commands sent to real machinery.*
> - *Human Oversight: The system mandates human supervisor intervention whenever anomalies arise."*

## 4:30 – 5:00 | Conclusion & Impact
> *"OrePulse does not attempt to replace mining professionals with AI. It empowers the next generation of Zambian mining talent to understand, evaluate, and safely work alongside agentic AI. Thank you."*
